# HomeTiles v0.6.4

v0.6.4 improves long-term stability and responsiveness and adds configurable
local hardware I/O.

## Upgrade Order

1. Update **HomeTiles Bridge to v0.6.32** through HACS and restart Home Assistant.
2. Update the display to HomeTiles v0.6.4.

## Highlights

- Faster folder navigation, Weather popups and tile restoration, including
  during long sessions.
- Light brightness, color and Kelvin sliders reliably send the newest value and
  both endpoints while remaining rate-limited.
- Separate screensaver brightness and more reliable idle handling, including
  open Camera popups.
- New **Hardware** tab for up to eight local switches, onboard relays or DS18B20
  sensors. Local tiles work directly on the panel; Bridge v0.6.32 adds the
  entities to Home Assistant.
- Faster web-admin loading and refresh behavior.
- Improved ESP-Hosted RPC/SDIO recovery and diagnostics. The safe-restart
  fallback remains for unresolved upstream WiFi/C6 lockups.

Camera support on ESP32-P4 remains **experimental**. The Guition
ESP32-4848S040 ESP32-S3 image also remains **experimental** and does not support
Camera tiles.

Hardware-confirmed targets are M5Stack Tab5, Waveshare 4B/86 Panel/8-inch and
Guition JC8012. Waveshare 7/10.1-inch, Guition JC1060 and Guition ESP32-S3 remain
community-test images.

Use the regular `.bin` for OTA and `_factory.bin` only for the first USB flash
or a complete reset.
